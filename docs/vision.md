# Product Vision

**Project Name:** BookBite *(Working Title)*

---

# 1. Vision Statement

BookBite helps readers capture meaningful ideas and summaries from books without interrupting their reading flow. By making note-taking effortless and natural during reading, BookBite allows users to gradually build a personal archive of their reading journey.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Reading books often creates moments of insight, important ideas, and meaningful passages that readers want to remember. However, traditional note-taking methods interrupt the reading experience and force readers to leave the mental space of the book.

Writing summaries by hand requires significant effort and time, while digital note-taking often creates friction and distraction. As a result, many readers either stop taking notes or lose valuable thoughts and reflections from the books they read.

BookBite solves this problem by allowing readers to capture important passages quickly, preserve them with minimal interruption, and organize them later when the reading session has ended.

---

# 3. Target Users

## Primary Users

BookBite is designed for readers who enjoy books and want to preserve meaningful ideas, passages, and personal summaries from the books they read.

This includes both passionate readers and casual readers who want to remember, reflect on, and revisit their reading experiences without interrupting the process of reading.

These users see books as personal experiences and want to build a meaningful record of their journey through books.

## Secondary Users

Students, researchers, and knowledge-focused readers may also benefit from BookBite for capturing information from books, but academic research workflows are not the primary focus of the product.

---

# 4. Core Value Proposition

BookBite removes the friction between reading and note-taking.

Instead of interrupting the reading experience to write summaries manually, readers can instantly capture important passages from their books and refine them later. This allows them to stay immersed in the book while gradually building a meaningful personal reading archive.

---

# 5. Product Experience Philosophy

BookBite should feel like a personal reading companion and a beautiful private library.

The product is not only a tool for storing information; it is a place where readers preserve their relationship with books and create a personal history of what they have read.

The experience should feel calm, immersive, and personal. The application should support the reading experience rather than compete with it.

---

# 6. Core Product Feeling

BookBite should feel like owning a personal library that grows with every book a user reads.

It should provide the calm and focused feeling of a reading companion while helping users preserve their personal journey through books.

---

# 7. Core User Journey

1. The user installs BookBite and immediately gains access to the core reading experience without being required to create an account.

2. The user discovers a book through the catalog or manually creates one if it is unavailable.

3. The user adds books to their personal library whenever they choose.

4. When ready, the user starts a new reading cycle for a selected book.

5. The user may optionally start a Focus Reading Session to create a distraction-free reading environment and record reading activity.

6. While reading, the user captures important pages using the device camera. The application extracts text using the most appropriate OCR method (local or cloud-based) while preserving the reading flow.

7. Captured text is stored as editable book notes, allowing the user to continue reading immediately.

8. After finishing the reading session, the user reviews, edits, and organizes captured notes at their own pace.

9. As more books are read, BookBite gradually builds a personal library and reading history that reflects the user's journey through books.

10. When the user chooses to create an account, local data can be synchronized with the cloud without changing the core reading experience.

---

# 8. MVP Definition

The MVP validates BookBite's core value: helping readers capture book notes without interrupting their reading experience.

The MVP includes:

- Browse and search books through an external catalog.
- Manually create books when they are unavailable.
- Build a personal book library.
- Manage book status (Want to Read, Reading, Finished).
- Support multiple reading cycles for the same book.
- Start and end Focus Reading Sessions.
- Capture book pages using the device camera.
- Convert captured pages into editable text using OCR.
- Store all core reading data locally for offline use.
- Support cloud synchronization when connectivity is available.
- Edit and organize captured notes after reading.
- Optional account creation and login.

The MVP intentionally prioritizes simplicity, reliability, and an uninterrupted reading experience over advanced features.

---

# 9. Future Direction

BookBite is designed to evolve beyond note capture into a complete personal reading companion.

Possible future capabilities include:

- AI-assisted summarization and note refinement.
- Smart OCR correction.
- Sticky notes, highlights, and rich annotations.
- Reading insights and personal statistics.
- Multi-device synchronization.
- Beautiful personal bookshelves.
- Public and private reading profiles.
- Community features inspired by Letterboxd.
- Book recommendations and discovery.
- Reading goals and achievements.

---

# 10. Non-Goals

The MVP is not intended to become:

- An academic research or citation management tool.
- A general-purpose note-taking application.
- A productivity platform competing with Notion, Obsidian, or Evernote.
- An AI-first product where automation replaces the reader's own thinking.
- A social network centered around engagement metrics rather than reading.

---

# 11. Product Identity

BookBite is a **reading-first** application.

OCR, AI, cloud synchronization, and future social features exist to support the reading experience—not to replace or distract from it.

Every design and engineering decision should reinforce one central idea:

> **Help people preserve what matters from the books they read without taking them out of the world they are immersed in.**
